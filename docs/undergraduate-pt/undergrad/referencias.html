Referências bibliográficas

Becker, Gary S. 1968. “Crime and Punishment: An Economic Approach.” Journal of Political Economy Vol. 76 (No. 2): pp. 169–217.
Blattman, Christopher, Donald P Green, Daniel Ortega, and Santiago Tobón. 2021. “Place-Based Interventions at Scale: The Direct and Spillover Effects of Policing and City Services on Crime.” Journal of the European Economic Association Vol. 19 (No. 4): pp. 2022–2051.
Braga, Anthony A, Brandon Turchan, Andrew V Papachristos, and David M Hureau. 2019. “Hot Spots Policing of Small Geographic Areas Effects on Crime.” Campbell Systematic Reviews Vol. 15 (No. 3): e1046.
Britto, Diogo GC, Paolo Pinotti, and Breno Sampaio. 2022. “The Effect of Job Loss and Unemployment Insurance on Crime in Brazil.” Econometrica Vol. 90 (No. 4): pp. 1393–1423.

Engelhardt, Bryan, Guillaume Rocheteau, and Peter Rupert. 2008. “Crime and the Labor Market: A Search Model with Optimal Contracts.” Journal of Public Economics Vol. 92 (No. 10-11): pp. 1876–1891.
Evans, William N, and Emily G Owens. 2007. “COPS and Crime.” Journal of Public Economics Vol. 91 (No. 1-2): pp. 181–201.
Glaeser, Edward L, Bruce Sacerdote, and Jose A Scheinkman. 1996. “Crime and Social Interactions.” The Quarterly Journal of Economics Vol. 111 (No. 2): pp. 507–548.
Gollier, Christian. 2001. “The Expected Utility Model.” In The Economics of Risk and Time, 3–16. Cambridge, MA: The MIT Press.

Koppensteiner, Martin Foureaux, and Livia Menezes. 2021. “Violence and Human Capital Investments.” Journal of Labor Economics Vol. 39 (No. 3): pp. 787–823.
Lee, David S, and Justin McCrary. 2017. “The Deterrence Effect of Prison: Dynamic Theory and Evidence.” In Regression Discontinuity Designs: Theory and Applications, 38:73–146. Advances in Econometrics. Emerald Group Publishing Limited.
Levitt, Steven D. 1997. “Using Electoral Cycles in Police Hiring to Estimate the Effect of Police on Crime.” American Economic Review Vol. 87 (No. 3): pp. 270–290.
Lochner, Lance. 2007. “Individual Perceptions of the Criminal Justice System.” American Economic Review Vol. 97 (No. 1): pp. 444–460.
Lochner, Lance, and Enrico Moretti. 2004. “The Effect of Education on Crime: Evidence from Prison Inmates, Arrests, and Self-Reports.” American Economic Review Vol. 94 (No. 1): pp. 155–189.
Machin, Stephen, Olivier Marie, and Sunčica Vujić. 2011. “The Crime Reducing Effect of Education.” The Economic Journal Vol. 121 (No. 552): pp. 463–484.
Mastrobuoni, Giovanni. 2019. “Police Disruption and Performance: Evidence from Recurrent Redeployments Within a City.” Journal of Public Economics Vol. 176: pp. 18–31.
McCrary, Justin. 2002. “Using Electoral Cycles in Police Hiring to Estimate the Effect of Police on Crime: Comment.” American Economic Review Vol. 92 (No. 4): pp. 1236–1243.

Cheltenham, UK: Edward Elgar Publishing Limited.
Mello, Steven. 2019. “More COPS, Less Crime.” Journal of Public Economics Vol. 172: pp. 174–200.

Pinotti, Paolo. 2020. “The Credibility Revolution in the Empirical Analysis of Crime.” Italian Economic Journal Vol. 6: pp. 207–220.
Pyle, David J. 1983. “The Economic Theory of Criminal Behaviour.” In The Economics of Crime and Law Enforcement, 8–28. London, UK: The MacMillan Press.
